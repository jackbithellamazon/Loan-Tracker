Repayment Sweet Spot — Offer Slider
Lenders label an offer "7 months", but the month label is not what you repay. Move the repayment and this works out the real schedule — how many payment dates there actually are, how big the last one is, and the exact day it finishes. A payment-date cliff is the point where paying a little more each fortnight deletes a whole future payment date. Everything here is saved in your browser.
