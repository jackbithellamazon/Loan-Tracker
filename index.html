Repayment Sweet Spot — Offer Slider
For choosing the repayment on your next advance. Lenders quote a month term; that is not what you repay. Move the slider and this works out the real schedule — how many payment dates there are, how many weeks it runs, how big the last payment is and the exact day it finishes. A payment-date cliff is where paying slightly more deletes a whole future payment date. Pin up to three levels to compare them. Saved in your browser.
Paste your offer
Select the whole offer panel on the lender's site and paste it here — advance, remittance, fee, total, deployment date and their balance table if it's there. Everything below fills in and the schedule is worked out for you. Their month label is noted for reference only; it never enters the maths.
